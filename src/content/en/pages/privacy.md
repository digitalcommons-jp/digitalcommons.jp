## Our policy

We do not collect any information about visitors, including metadata. We do not use cookies. We do not load any external services, fonts, scripts, or ads. For event registration and contact forms, every message reaches the operator's device in encrypted form, without being decrypted along the way. Messages are used only to reply, and are never provided to third parties without the sender's consent. Upon the sender's request, we will promptly disclose, correct, or delete the content they sent us.

## Intermediary providers

This website uses a few providers. We use Cloudflare to resolve the domain name. Visitors' traffic reaches GitHub Pages. Together with the visitor's IP address, these providers may be able to see the following information.

| Role | Provider | Information they can see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domain name resolution (DNS) | Cloudflare | Queries for the domain name |
| Site delivery | GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) | The traffic itself |

If a DDoS attack or similar causes a disruption, the traffic itself may temporarily pass through Cloudflare in order to keep the service running. We will announce this on the website while it is the case.
