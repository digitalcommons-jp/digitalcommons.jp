## Anyone can join, no conditions

Offline, we are currently active mainly in the Kanto region of Japan. Online, we keep in touch by chat and email. Membership is free. No specialized knowledge or skills are required, and beginners are very welcome. You are even welcome to just peek into the chat anonymously. Are you interested in any of the following topics?

`Anti-war` `Digital rights` `Data protection & GDPR` `Freedom of expression` `Data sovereignty` `Open source` `Cybersecurity` `Encryption` `Decentralization & federation` `Digital homelessness` `Digital well-being` `Surveillance capitalism` `Digital colonization` `Digital divide` `Luddite movement` `Anti-platform` `Local-first` `Indie web` 

## To other civil society organizations

Information and communication technology has permeated every part of our daily activities. Yet much of it remains a black box. As a result, we unknowingly take on all kinds of risks and contradictions. Won't you join us in solidarity to rethink how we live with these technologies? Please feel free to join us or to ask us anything.
