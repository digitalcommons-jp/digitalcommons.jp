(Placeholder) Smartphones, computers, routers, smart speakers... There are probably more things than you would expect that you use every day without knowing what goes on inside.

In this workshop, each participant brings their own "black box". It can be a device, an app, or a service. You become aware of just how much of what you use every day is beyond your understanding, and talk about it with the other participants.

## How the day goes

1. Introduce what you brought and put into words "what I don't understand about it"
2. Line up the black boxes together by how opaque they are
3. Take a little peek inside (settings, network traffic, the flow of data, and so on)
4. Talk about what you noticed

## How to take part

No experience is needed. You can take part even if you have not decided what to bring. Please register using "Apply" below.
